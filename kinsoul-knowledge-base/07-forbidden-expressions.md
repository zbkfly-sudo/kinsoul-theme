# 博客禁用表述完整清单(2026-04-18 定)

> 这是 Hermes 自检的硬门槛之一。任何一篇博客扫出以下表述必须重写。

## 时间欺骗类
- "4 years of experience / craftsmanship / sourcing"
- "Years of" + 任何未验证年数
- "Over the years"
- "We've been doing this for a long time"
- "Our longtime customers"

## 虚假销售数据类
- "Our best-selling"(首月数据不够)
- "Our most popular"
- "Thousands of customers love..."
- "Processed X pearls" / "Made X bracelets"
- "Sold out"(除非真的卖光)

## 虚假客户原话类(我们只有总结,没有原话)
- "An early customer wrote: '...'"
- "One customer told us: '...'"
- "A customer said: '...'"
- 任何带引号的具体客户原话

**允许的替代**:
- "Early customer feedback has focused on..."
- "The themes we've been hearing most often are..."

## 伪科学类(品牌调性禁区)
- "Energy healing"
- "Spiritual protection"
- "Crystal power"
- "Chakra balancing"
- "Aura cleansing"(Aura 作产品名 OK,不用作玄学概念)
- "Vibration / frequency"
- "Miracle / cure / heal / fix"
- "Divine / sacred"
- "Wards off negative energy"
- "Anxiety relief"(医疗声称,FTC 禁)

## 夸张类(Kinsoul voice 禁)
- "Amazing / incredible / unbelievable"
- "Revolutionary / groundbreaking"
- "Best in the world / unrivaled / the only"
- "Stunning / breathtaking / gorgeous"(过度形容)
- "Perfect"(克制原则)

## 合规类(FTC / GIA 限制)
- "100% natural"(除非有证明文件)
- "Certified"(除非有证书)
- "Ethically sourced"(除非有完整供应链追溯)
- "Fair trade"(除非有认证)
- "Conflict-free"(除非有追溯)
- "Handmade in America"(我们是加州工作室,可以写"Hand-assembled in California";但石头和珍珠来自国外)
- "Pure silver"(应写 S925 或 Sterling Silver)

## 定位错位类(不装老牌)
- "Heritage brand"
- "Established in..."(我们 2019 才创立,也就 6 年品牌)
- "Iconic / legendary"
- "Our signature collection"(对小品牌来说显得过头)

## 石头名错误类(宝石学准确性)
- ❌ Obsidian 叫 "Rutilated Quartz" → ✅ "Tourmalinated Quartz"
- ❌ Soleil 叫 "Yellow Quartz"(没用 Citrine 的统一名) → ✅ "Citrine"
- ❌ Aura 说 "8 gemstones" 不带说明 → ✅ "8 gemstones selected from a curated palette of 12 natural stones"
